# AI and CS Education

What should CS students learn these days, and how should they learn it? Here some observations from the session.

* The basics are still important. Students must be able to read code and reason about it. They must study algorithms, hardware performance, concurrency, security, and software engineering.
* Programming language minutiae not so much if most code is AI generated.
* Learning has never been better for self-motivated students who can ask the AI to check solutions and propose appropriate challenges. How can we get more students to take advantage of that?
* Testing has become hugely important, and now we have AI assistance to help generate and execute tests. This should be taught and used much earlier than before.
* Architecture and design are important, but how do we teach them effectively? Asking humdrum questions about design patterns isn't going to work when the bots have all the “answers”.
* In past project courses, there were too many greenfield projects. Now with AI, it is easy to start with an instant brownfield.
* Classic coding challenges have become useless since the AI handily delivers instant solutions. Interview students, have them explain, and make them do instant changes.
* At least that's the theory. In practice, as a participant working for a major corporation pointed out, there are long-standing vendor relationships for job interviews. Job candidates will have to do classic coding challenges for some time. Or get good at cheating their way through them.
